# Demo video plan

Target length: **1:45–2:15**. Maximum allowed length: **3:00**.

Record the Lens Studio Preview without the LEAF panel or a LEAF result banner visible. Use the SPECS 27 Interactive Preview.

## Shot list and narration

### 0:00–0:15 — Problem

> When you see an interesting building, finding its available apartments usually means stopping, searching for the address, and matching phone listings back to the façade. Street Property Information keeps that context in your view.

Show the street and translucent building stand-in with the orange `3` marker.

### 0:15–0:35 — Cluster

> The marker tells me that three apartments are available in this building. The overlay appears only while the building is in view.

Move the camera slightly, aim at the orange `3`, and click it.

### 0:35–1:00 — Floor markers

> One click or pinch expands the cluster into spatial markers for the 1st, 3rd, and 8th floors.

Pause so all three markers are clearly visible.

### 1:00–1:30 — Listing details

> Selecting a floor opens the listing beside that apartment. This sample is for sale, has three rooms, two bathrooms, and is priced at seven hundred eighty thousand dollars.

Click the `1` marker. Let the card remain visible, then click **CLOSE**.

### 1:30–1:50 — Spatial behavior

> Looking or walking away hides the overlay, so it stays attached to the task instead of becoming permanent HUD clutter.

Turn away or move beyond the visibility range, then return.

### 1:50–2:15 — Process and learning

> I built the interaction with Spectacles Interaction Kit and UI Kit and wrote five LEAF scenarios for the full flow. All five pass in Preview. The biggest lesson was that spatial UI needs to fit the field of view, remain within interaction range, and use bright contrast for an additive display. Real recognition and on-device comfort are the next steps.

End on the expanded markers or listing card.

## Recording checklist

- Reset the Preview camera before recording.
- Confirm the orange `3` marker is visible.
- Hide the LEAF panel and Logger panel.
- Record at 1080p if possible.
- Keep the cursor movements deliberate.
- Avoid long pauses and debugging UI.
- Upload as **Public** on YouTube.
- Add the final URL to `README.md` and `devpost/submission-checklist.md`.
