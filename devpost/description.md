# Short description

Street Property Information is a Specs AR concept that displays available apartments directly on the building a user is viewing. A numbered marker reveals listings at their approximate floor positions, and users can open property details without stopping to search for an address on their phone.

It is designed for pedestrians, renters, and homebuyers exploring a neighborhood. Building recognition and listing data are simulated in this prototype, while the complete spatial interaction flow is implemented with Spectacles Interaction Kit and Spectacles UI Kit.

I learned that spatial interfaces require careful balancing of world alignment, interaction distance, field of view, and additive-display contrast. LEAF testing proved the application logic, while comfort, real-world recognition, and hand-tracking feel still require validation on Specs hardware.

## One-line version

See available apartments directly on the building you are viewing, expand them by floor, and inspect a listing without taking out your phone.
